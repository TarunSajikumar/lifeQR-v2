## 📋 University Formatting Compliance Summary

Every document in this directory adheres strictly to the guidelines specified in the official project manual:

1. **Page Setup**:
   - Paper Size: A4 Bond Paper ($210 \times 297\text{ mm}$).
   - Margins: Left = $1.25''$ ($31.75\text{ mm}$ for binding gutter), Right = $1.0''$ ($25.4\text{ mm}$), Top & Bottom = $0.75''$ ($19.05\text{ mm}$).
   - Line Spacing: Exactly 1.5 line spacing.
   - Text Alignment: Justified ($12\text{ pt}$).
2. **Typography Hierarchy**:
   - Chapter Number & Label: $16\text{ pt}$ (Left-to-Right).
   - Chapter Title: $18\text{ pt}$ Bold (Centered).
   - Major Sections ($1.1, 2.1\dots$): $16\text{ pt}$ Bold (Left-aligned).
   - Subsections ($1.1.1, 2.1.2\dots$): $14\text{ pt}$ Semi-bold (Left-aligned).
   - Body Text: $12\text{ pt}$ Justified.
3. **Numbering Convention**:
   - Preliminary Pages (Title, Certificate, Abstract, Acknowledgments, Contents, Lists of Tables/Figures): Lowercase Roman numerals (`i, ii, iii, iv\dots`).
   - Main Body Chapters ($1\dots 8$): Arabic numerals with decimal subsectioning ($2.1, 2.2, 2.2.3\dots$).
   - Equations: Right-justified decimal notation e.g., $(3.1), (3.2)$.
   - Figures & Tables: Chapter-wise notation e.g., `Fig. 1.1`, `Table 3.1`.
4. **Abstract Limitation**: Strictly $\le 100$ words summarizing the salient features of the system.
5. **Mandatory Appendices**:
   - **Appendix A (Hardware Project Details)**: NFC circuit layout, component bill of materials (BOM), coil inductance, and NTAG215/PN532 datasheets.
   - **Appendix B (Software Project Details)**: Disc layout, complete environment variable dictionary, Node.js compilation/runtime steps, software requirements, and public domain library disclosures.
6. **Hard Binding & Copies**:
   - 1 Copy: Department.
   - 1 Copy: Guide(s).
   - 2 Copies: Sponsoring Agency (if applicable).
   - 1 Copy: Candidate.
   - Dry toner Xerox recommended; hard-bound with plastic transparent covering.
